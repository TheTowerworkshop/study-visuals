# Engineering AI Governance: From Policy to Proof

## The central idea

Many organizations treat AI governance as a paperwork exercise: write the policies, form a committee, fill out a risk register. Those pieces are needed, but they are not enough. A policy that never becomes a control is just a statement of intent, and a control that is never tested is just an assumption.

This series looks at AI governance as an **engineered capability** with three jobs:

1. **Translate** organizational intent into technical and procedural controls
2. **Validate** that those controls work as designed
3. **Provide evidence** that they are effective, in a form that leadership, auditors, and regulators can rely on
